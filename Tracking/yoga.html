THE PRACTICE

Yoga & Its Asanas

Yoga unites breath, body, and attention. Each asana below is a doorway — hold it softly, breathe steadily, and let the shape teach you.

TREE POSE

Vrksasana

This pose is a standing balance posture that builds stillness and focus. Rooted through one leg, the sole of the other foot presses into the inner thigh or calf, strengthening balance and concentration, and cultivating a sense of grounded calm.

WARRIOR II

Virabhadrasana II

Warrior II opens the hips and chest, building stamina and stability in the legs. With arms extended wide and gaze steady over the front hand, this pose embodies strength, confidence, and mindful presence — a powerful reminder that calm and courage can coexist.

DOWNWARD DOG

Adho Mukha Svanasana

An essential inversion that stretches the hamstrings, shoulders, and calves while strengthening the upper body. Downward Dog energizes the whole body and brings fresh oxygenated blood to the brain.

COBRA POSE

Bhujangasana

Bhujangasana is a gentle backbend that opens the heart, increases spinal flexibility, and strengthens the spine and abdominal organs. It relieves fatigue and stress while lifting the mood.

TRIANGLE POSE

Trikonasana

Trikonasana stretches the legs, knees, ankles, arms, and chest while expanding the lungs. It improves physical balance, digestion, and aids in relieving stress and backache.

CHILD'S POSE

Balasana

A restorative resting posture that gently stretches the hips, thighs, and ankles while calming the mind. Balasana helps relieve stress, tension in the back, and promotes deep conscious breathing.

BRIDGE POSE

Setu Bandhasana

Bridge pose opens the chest, heart, and shoulders while strengthening the back, glutes, and hamstrings. It stimulates thyroid glands, improves digestion, and helps alleviate stress.

LOTUS POSE

Padmasana

The classic cross-legged meditative posture that promotes deep mental tranquility, opens the hips, and aligns the spine. Padmasana encourages stillness and prepares the mind for pranayama.

EAGLE POSE

Garudasana

Garudasana requires acute concentration and steady balance. Wrapping the arms and legs stretches the shoulders, upper back, and thighs while sharpening mental focus.

CORPSE POSE

Savasana

The final resting pose of every yoga session. Savasana allows the body to integrate the benefits of practice, releases nervous system tension, and restores total harmony to body and mind.
